in 2 minutes.

TimbSwap runs on the Arbitrum Sepolia testnet — everything below is free and uses no real money. Follow the four steps and you'll be in your first round.

  1. 1

    Get a wallet + add the network

    Install MetaMask (or any EVM wallet), then add the Arbitrum Sepolia testnet in one click at chainlist.org ↗.

    Already have a wallet? Just switch it to Arbitrum Sepolia and skip ahead.

  2. 2

    Grab free test gas (ETH)

    You need a little Arbitrum Sepolia ETH to pay gas — it's free from a public faucet. Try the Alchemy or QuickNode testnet faucets and send it to your wallet address.

    Testnet ETH has no value — it just covers network fees so your trades go through.

  3. 3

    Get your TIMBS

    Head to the TimbSwap faucet, connect your wallet, and claim free testnet TIMBS to play with.

    Open the faucet →
  4. 4

    Enter a round

    Set a 6-character string, then trade. Every eligible swap nudges the live meter — if the round locks on your six, you split the pot. The prize is funded by yield, so what you put in stays yours.
