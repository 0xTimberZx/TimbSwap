    Details

    Head to Compete, connect, and set a 6-character ticket (A–Z, 0–9, no repeats) for 0.001 ETH. The deposit is escrowed, not spent: it stays yours while the ticket plays, and you claim it back within six rounds (about three days) of its last round, or it lapses. Up to six extra rounds are free during the beta.

    Set a 6-character string, then trade. Swaps nudge the meter; if the round locks on your six, you split the pot. The pot comes from yield, so your deposit stays yours: claim it back within six rounds of your last one.
