Why TimbSwap — and not just another DEX?

A proven AMM at the core

Under the hood it's a Uniswap-V2-style constant-product exchange — battle-tested pool mechanics in open-source Solidity, verified on-chain, nothing exotic where your liquidity lives.

Trades do more than trade

On a plain DEX a swap just swaps. Here every eligible swap also plays: it pushes the live meter toward a pot that pays winners from yield — not from anyone's deposit.

Rewards are earned, not printed

Emissions replenish only from real treasury inflow, under a fixed 100M supply cap and a solvency stop at 99% of obligations. There is no inflation faucet.

A price gap opens, the fastest bot takes it, and the edge is gone in a block. One winner, once.

THE TIMBSWAP SPREAD

Rewarded volume, funded emissions, and prize odds — an edge that resets every six rounds, open to anyone, not just the fastest machine.

EVEN ON A FLAT DAY

The edge is generated, not discovered.

Classic arbitrage needs a dislocation — it only exists when volume or volatility opens a gap. TimbSwap's edge runs on the protocol's clock, not the market's, so opportunity persists when the tape is quiet.

  • Emissions still flow. The epoch sweep runs every six rounds regardless of volume — LP and staking rewards don't need volatility to appear.
  • The round still turns. Segments lock on a timer; on a slow day, fewer players nudge the meter, so the ones who show up face better odds on the pot.
  • The pot still grows. Deposited capital earns yield in the vault whether or not anyone trades, so the prize accrues on flat days too.
  • Thin volume still compounds. Whatever trading does happen routes through your liquidity and refills the treasury that feeds the waterfall.
THE RISKS

What you're taking on.

  • Impermanent loss. Providing liquidity carries IL — larger on thin or volatile pairs (boosted farms are the highest-risk seat).
  • Emissions vary. The rate moves with the waterfall and total staked; if inflow runs short the tail (boost) can go unfunded. Testnet APR is illustrative, not a promise.
  • The game is chance-weighted. You aim a segment's class, not the exact character — the pot is never guaranteed.
  • Token & liquidity exposure. TIMBS rewards carry TIMBS price risk; staked positions are locked while staked.
  • Unaudited testnet. Contracts are unaudited and every asset is test-only, with no monetary value.
