Overview

TimbSwap runs on Arbitrum Sepolia testnet: every token is a test asset with no monetary value, and the contracts are unaudited. This bounty runs now — before the independent audit and any mainnet launch — so vulnerabilities get found and fixed while nothing real is at stake. The contracts are open-source in contracts/ and their deployed testnet addresses are in the Docs address table.

Report privately — do not open a public issue. Prefer your own local fork; don't grief, spam, or DoS the shared Arbitrum Sepolia deployment, the faucet, or the keeper that other players and hunters rely on. Severity is judged by the impact a bug would have with real funds, so a drain-class finding is rewarded as such even though it's caught on testnet. Bands and cap rise for the mainnet program after the audit.

Rewards

Severity is impact-based — what the bug would do with real funds — guided by the Immunefi severity classification. Rewards are paid in ETH or USDC to the first reporter of a unique issue, from the testnet hardening pool. No single payout exceeds $500 in this pre-mainnet phase.

Scope

The deployed testnet contracts in the Docs address table and their source in contracts/.

In scope

  • DEX core — TimbSwapFactory, TimbSwapRouter, the TIMBS/WETH pair.
  • Prize game — TimbPrize, GameRegistry, PrizeEscrow, VRFEntropy, EligibleTokenRegistry.
  • Token & incentives — TIMBSToken, TimbStaking, TimbFarm, TimbBoostFarm, TimbLockVault, TimbYieldVault, TimbTreasury.
  • Governance — TimbGovernance, TimelockController.
  • A frontend bug that can misdirect or drain funds (wrong address in config.js, an unbounded approval, chain/network confusion).

Out of scope

  • The frontend / static site for cosmetic issues; off-chain keeper / telemetry infra.
  • Third-party code / infra: Chainlink VRF, OpenZeppelin, the Arbitrum sequencer / bridge, RPC providers, wallets.
  • Already-documented behavior (see SECURITY.md): FoT / rebasing unsupported; timelock-gated admin powers; read-only reentrancy on getReserves().
  • Wallet connection problems, gas-golf, and scanner output without a working PoC.
  • Spam / DoS against shared testnet infra — the public RPC, the faucet, or the keeper. That's griefing, not a vulnerability.

How to report

1 · Send a private report

Primary channel: GitHub → the repo → Security → “Report a vulnerability” (private advisory). Include the affected contract(s) + address, a description, the impact, and a proof of concept — a Foundry test or fork script is ideal. A backup security contact is in SECURITY.md.

Open a private advisory ↗︎

2 · We acknowledge within 72 hours, triage severity, and keep you updated through the fix.

3 · Coordinated disclosure — please give us up to 90 days (or until a fix ships) before any public disclosure.

Rules of engagement & safe harbor

  • Prefer your own local fork. Where you touch the shared Arbitrum Sepolia deployment, keep it to a minimal, non-destructive PoC — never grief, spam, or DoS it, the faucet, or the keeper.
  • Never touch other users' data or interfere with other players' testnet activity beyond what a PoC needs.
  • No social engineering, phishing, physical attacks, or targeting of team members or third parties.
  • One report per unique root cause; don't spam variants.
Safe harbor. For research conducted in good faith and within these rules, we will not pursue or support legal action against you, and we consider your activity authorized. This is not authorization to violate any law or to access accounts/data that are not yours. If in doubt, ask first via the private channel.

Not open to current or former TimbSwap contributors or their immediate family. You must comply with applicable sanctions / AML law; KYC may be required before payout.

Why hunt now

This is the ground-floor moment. Every bug surfaced on testnet is one that never reaches mainnet — and the hunters who show up early get more than the payout:

  • Get paid now. Real ETH / USDC for issues caught while nothing is at real risk — a rare bounty you can hunt without racing a live exploit.
  • Priority allowlist. Researchers get early scope updates and first look at the expanded mainnet program — bigger bands and cap — once the audit clears.
  • Recognition. The first valid reporter of a unique issue is credited; the reputation carries into the mainnet program.
