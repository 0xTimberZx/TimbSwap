How it works

Sit with a six-character ticket and take your six segment tokens — one per segment. First load every token with a TIMBS chip: all six carry a stake before the board opens to you. Then place them — pick a spot, read what it calls for, approve the transaction. Once it's mined, it's final. Tokens you load but never place come back as withdrawable credit when the table retires. Operating a table (open / lock / retire) lives in the console.

    Every placement is a wallet approval, and once mined it can't be undone. Payout is pari-mutuel: each of the seven pools (six segments + Repeats a Digit) forms its own pot — every chip in the pool — and when the round locks its six characters, the pot minus rake splits among winners in proportion to stake × fair-multiple. The printed odds (36 ÷ symbols − 1) are relative weights, not a fixed house payout; the actual return floats with how the table bet. Rake is graduated: 8% on an uncontested pool, falling toward 1.75% as more wallets join (4.87% at two). Losing chips fund the winners; nobody banks against the table. Even/odd doesn't exist here — a letter-and-number alphabet has no parity.
